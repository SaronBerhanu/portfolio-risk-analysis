# Historical Return and Risk Analysis

An Excel-based analysis comparing the historical returns and volatility of SPY, AGG, and Microsoft.

## Overview

This project evaluates the risk-and-return characteristics of three different investments:

- **SPY:** Broad U.S. stock market ETF
- **AGG:** U.S. investment-grade bond ETF
- **MSFT:** Individual technology stock

Historical adjusted closing prices were used to calculate monthly returns, average returns, and volatility. Monthly results were then annualized to make the investments easier to compare.

## Methodology

- Collected historical adjusted closing prices
- Calculated monthly returns from changes in adjusted prices
- Calculated average monthly returns
- Measured monthly volatility using the standard deviation of returns
- Annualized average returns by multiplying monthly returns by 12
- Annualized volatility by multiplying monthly volatility by the square root of 12

## Results

| Investment | Average Monthly Return | Annualized Return | Monthly Volatility | Annualized Volatility |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SPY | 1.13% | 13.50% | 4.45% | 15.43% |
| AGG | 0.01% | 0.14% | 1.90% | 6.57% |
| MSFT | 1.31% | 15.74% | 6.33% | 21.94% |

## Key Findings

- **MSFT** generated the highest historical return but also had the highest volatility.
- **AGG** had the lowest volatility, but its historical return was close to zero.
- **SPY** provided a stronger balance between return and volatility than the individual stock.
- The analysis demonstrates the risk-return tradeoff: investments with higher historical returns also experienced greater price fluctuations.
